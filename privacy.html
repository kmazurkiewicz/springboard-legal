1Introductory Provisions and Scope

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") sets forth the principles and conditions governing the collection, storage, processing, and transfer of Personal Data of Users of the SpringBoard mobile application (the "Application"). The Policy has been prepared in compliance with:

  • Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data ("GDPR");
  • Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 19 October 2022 on a Single Market For Digital Services ("Digital Services Act" / "DSA");
  • Applicable provisions of Polish civil law; and
  • Platform policies of Google Play Console and Apple App Store.

This Policy applies to all natural persons who install, access, or otherwise use the Application, irrespective of their jurisdiction of residence, to the extent permitted by applicable mandatory law.

4Categories of Personal Data Collected

4.1 Identification and Authentication Data

  • Electronic mail address (e-mail), used as the primary Account identifier;
  • Cryptographic hash of the User's password (stored exclusively in hashed form; the Controller does not retain plaintext passwords);
  • Account creation timestamp and last-authentication metadata.

4.2 Anthropometric and Training Data

  • Body mass, height, and derived biometric parameters (e.g., body mass index) where voluntarily provided;
  • Exercise logs: exercise names, sets, repetitions, loads (in kilograms or pounds), and rest intervals;
  • User-defined workout plan architectures, training blocks, and periodisation schemes;
  • Dynamic indicators of central nervous system (CNS) fatigue and per-muscle-group recovery status, as calculated by the Application's algorithms on the basis of User-inputted training data.

4.3 AI Services Interaction Data

  • Textual content of conversational queries submitted to the AI Services (chat metadata);
  • AI-generated responses stored for continuity of the User session;
  • Aggregated usage metrics relating to the daily interaction volume limits applicable under the Free Plan and the Premium Plan.
Please do not enter into the AI chat any information that is not necessary to obtain training advice, in particular data concerning your health, medical conditions, or other sensitive matters, nor data of other persons. Content you type into the chat is transmitted to the AI Providers as described in Section 6.

4.4 Technical and Device Data

  • Anonymised or pseudonymised application session logs stored on Supabase infrastructure;
  • User interface cache data and query-limit identifiers stored in SharedPreferences / Local Storage on the User's device (persisted locally; not transmitted to the Controller's servers);
  • Application version, operating system type and version, and device locale settings.

4.5 Data Not Collected

The Controller expressly does not collect: precise geolocation data; payment card or banking details (subscription payments are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase, as applicable, and the Controller receives only a non-sensitive transaction confirmation); biometric identifiers within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR; or any data relating to minors under the age of sixteen (16).

7Data Retention Periods

Personal Data shall be retained for the following periods:

  • Account Data: for the duration of the active Account and for a period of three (3) years following Account deletion, unless a longer retention period is mandated by applicable law (e.g., tax obligations);
  • Training and Anthropometric Data: for the duration of the active Account; deleted within ninety (90) days of Account deletion upon User request;
  • AI Services Interaction Data (chat metadata): retained on Supabase servers for a rolling period of twelve (12) months from the date of each interaction, then permanently deleted;
  • Data held by AI Providers: retained by each AI Provider for the period stated in its terms and in the agreement with the Controller [state the period, if known]. The Controller does not store User Content with AI Providers beyond what is required to deliver a response, to the extent that this is within the Controller's control;

  • Data on Local Device Storage: persisted until the User uninstalls the Application or manually clears Application data through device settings.

Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, Personal Data shall be permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymised in a manner that prevents re-identification.

8Rights of Data Subjects

Pursuant to Chapter III GDPR, every User has the following rights with respect to their Personal Data:

  • Right of Access (Article 15 GDPR): the right to obtain confirmation as to whether Personal Data are being processed and, if so, to receive a copy thereof.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16 GDPR): the right to obtain rectification of inaccurate Personal Data and completion of incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Article 17 GDPR): the right to obtain erasure of Personal Data where one of the grounds specified in Article 17(1) GDPR applies.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18 GDPR): the right to obtain restriction of processing in the circumstances set out in Article 18(1) GDPR.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20 GDPR): the right to receive Personal Data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller, where processing is based on consent or contract.
  • Right to Object (Article 21 GDPR): the right to object, on grounds relating to the User's particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7(3) GDPR): where processing is based on consent, the right to withdraw such consent at any time, without prejudice to the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Office for Personal Data Protection (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland), or with the supervisory authority in the User's Member State of habitual residence.
To exercise any of the above rights, the User should submit a written request to the Controller's e-mail address stated in Section 3. The Controller shall respond within thirty (30) calendar days, or, in complex cases, within a maximum of ninety (90) calendar days, informing the User of any extension and the reasons therefor.

9Local Storage and Device-Side Data

The Application does not use browser-based cookies. However, it employs the following device-side storage mechanisms:

  • SharedPreferences (Android) / NSUserDefaults (iOS): used exclusively for storing user interface preferences (e.g., dark mode setting, unit system) and query-limit counters for rate-limiting purposes under the Free Plan. This data is strictly functional and does not constitute profiling or behavioural tracking.
  • In-Memory Cache: transient session data that is cleared upon Application closure and is never persisted to disk.

No third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking frameworks, or analytics SDKs that report Personal Data to external parties are embedded in the Application.

If the Controller becomes aware that Personal Data of a minor under the age of sixteen (16) has been collected without verifiable parental or guardian consent, the Controller shall promptly take steps to delete such data.

Parents or legal guardians who believe that their minor child has provided Personal Data to the Application without consent are requested to contact the Controller at the address specified in Section 3.

11Security Measures

The Controller implements appropriate technical and organisational measures, in accordance with Article 32 GDPR, to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:

  • Encryption (TLS 1.2 or higher) for all data in transit between the Application, the Supabase backend servers, and the AI Providers;
  • Storage of User passwords exclusively in the form of irreversible cryptographic hashes using industry-standard hashing algorithms (e.g., bcrypt/Argon2), as managed by Supabase Authentication;
  • Role-based access controls and row-level security policies (RLS) on the Supabase database layer, ensuring that each User can access exclusively their own data;
  • Data minimisation in communication with AI Providers: requests are routed through the Controller's server-side Edge Functions, API keys are never stored in the Application, and Account identifiers are not forwarded to AI Providers;
  • Regular security assessments of the Application's infrastructure;
  • Contractual obligations imposed on Processors to implement equivalent security standards, to the extent permitted by the provider's standard terms.

In the event of a Personal Data Breach that is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, the Controller shall notify the affected Users without undue delay, in accordance with Article 34 GDPR.
