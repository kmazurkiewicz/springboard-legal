Account & Data Deletion

Request Account & Data Deletion

If you want to permanently remove your SpringBoard account and all associated data, follow the steps below. This action cannot be undone.

Warning: Deleting your account will permanently erase your training history, profile, statistics, and all other data linked to your account.
How to request deletion
  1. Open your email client and create a new message to kmdevworks.contact@gmail.com.
  2. Use exactly this subject line: Delete account
  3. Send the email from the address linked to your SpringBoard account, so we can verify ownership.
  4. We will confirm your request and permanently delete your account and data within 14 days.
What gets deleted
ProfileUsername, avatar, and account credentials
TrainingAll workout sessions and exercise history
StatisticsProgress data, personal records, and analytics
SettingsApp preferences and notification settings
In-app deletion
  1. Open SpringBoard and go to Settings → Profile.
  2. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the page.
  3. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.
